## About

Dr. Habtom Desta Asfaw is a researcher at the **Department of Chemistry – Ångström Laboratory, Uppsala University**, where he specialises in **materials synthesis and electrode fabrication** for electrochemical energy-storage devices, including batteries and supercapacitors.

His work bridges fundamental electrochemistry and applied materials engineering — designing electrode architectures, probing electrode–electrolyte interfaces, and developing next-generation chemistries such as dual-ion and solid-state batteries.

Originally from Ethiopia, Dr. Asfaw obtained his PhD at Uppsala University, held postdoctoral positions at Imperial College London and Empa (Switzerland), and returned to Uppsala as a researcher in 2019.

---

## Research Interests

Beyond intrinsic material properties, the performance of an electrochemical device is governed by **electrode architecture** (active-material morphology, porosity, surface area) and the **stability of electrode–electrolyte interfaces**. Dr. Asfaw's current research focuses on:

1. **Engineering electrode microstructure** — building battery electrodes with controlled morphology and three-dimensional architectures to increase energy and power density.
2. **Understanding interfacial chemistry** — probing the nature and evolution of solid–electrolyte interphases in advanced cell chemistries.

### Topical areas

- Design and fabrication of **3D microstructured electrodes** for miniature energy-storage devices
- Synthesis of **carbon aerogel–reinforced carbon-fibre fabric electrodes** for structural supercapacitors
- Design and characterisation of **lithium- and potassium-based dual-ion batteries**
- Stability of **highly concentrated electrolytes (HCE)** at elevated voltages
- Stabilisation of the **graphite–electrolyte interface** in dual-ion batteries
- Conversion of **biomass-derived hard carbons** into anode materials for sodium-ion batteries

### Keywords

`Raman spectroscopy` · `electrochemistry` · `Li-, Na-, K-ion batteries` · `dual-ion batteries` · `carbon materials` · `hard carbon` · `graphite` · `supercapacitors`

### Characterisation expertise

Gas adsorption (BET) · Scanning and transmission electron microscopy · Raman and FTIR spectroscopy · X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) · Powder and grazing-incidence X-ray diffraction (XRD / GIXRD) · Galvanostatic & potentiostatic electrochemical methods
